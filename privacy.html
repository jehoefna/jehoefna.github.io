Privacy Policy – UndokQuick

Last updated: 2026-02-05

UndokQuick respects your privacy.

This app does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

What information is collected?

None.

UndokQuick:

  • Does not collect your name, email, or location
  • Does not track your usage
  • Does not use analytics
  • Does not show ads
  • Does not send any data to external servers

Network communication

The app communicates only with your Internet Radio device on your local Wi-Fi network in order to control it. No data is sent to UndokQuick servers or to any third party.

Permissions

The app may request:

  • Local network / Wi-Fi access – to find and control your radio

This is required for the app to work and is not used for anything else.

Third-party services

UndokQuick does not use any third-party services, SDKs, or trackers.

Children’s privacy

UndokQuick does not collect any data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this privacy policy changes, it will be updated on this page.
